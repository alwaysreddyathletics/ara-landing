Goal: PR Everything

How to Use This Log

Log Same DayFill in each day immediately after your session — don't wait. Details fade fast. The more honest your log entries, the more useful the data.
RPE ScaleRate every session 1–10. 1 = effortless walk. 10 = absolute maximum. Most sessions should be 6–7. If everything is 9–10, you're overtraining.
Weekly ReflectionWrite something — even two sentences. Patterns you can't see session-to-session become obvious over 4 weeks of written entries.
Monthly ReviewAt the end of each 4-week block, compare your numbers. This is where the progress becomes undeniable — and where you decide what to push next.

A Note from Coach Tyler

The Athlete Who Tracks Is the Athlete Who Improves.

I've coached athletes who trained hard and athletes who trained hard AND logged everything they did. The ones who log their training — the load, the sleep, the RPE, the reflection — they improve faster. Not because the log makes them fitter. Because it makes them self-aware, and self-aware athletes make better decisions week over week.

Twelve weeks of entries in this log will tell you more about yourself as an athlete than any personality test or fitness assessment. Use it. Fill it in honestly, especially on the bad days. Those are the entries that matter most.

— Coach Tyler Reddy · Always Reddy Athletics
