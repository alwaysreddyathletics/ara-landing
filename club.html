Questions

Straight answers

Is this 1-on-1 coaching?

No — the Club is group programming. Every month you get my full training system (running, strength, mobility) in two tracks — Speed (5K/10K) and Endurance (half/marathon); you pick yours and train alongside the whole Club. If you want a plan built specifically around you with direct check-ins, that's The Athletic Rebuild, my 1-on-1 coaching.

How much time does it take?

45–60 minutes a day, five to six days a week. Built for a person with a job, not a pro with a nap schedule.

I haven't trained seriously in years. Is this for me?

It's built for exactly you. Every program has an on-ramp tier, and the strength work scales from bodyweight up. You start where you are.

What happens after I join?

You're in the Club instantly — checkout and membership both live on Skool. The pinned Start Here post walks you through picking your track (Speed: 5K/10K, or Endurance: half/marathon), posting an intro, and starting this week's training. Cancel anytime in two clicks from your settings.
