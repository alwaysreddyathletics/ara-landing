FAQ

Straight answers.

I don't have a specific goal yet. Is this for me?

Yes. Pick "Move like an athlete again" and the plan builds general strength, conditioning, and engine. If a race or event comes up later, we point the block at it.

What if I have an injury or old injuries?

Tell me in the intake. The plan works around it with exercise swaps and adjusted volume. If you have a current injury, get cleared by a doctor or physical therapist before you start.

What equipment do I need? Gym or home?

Either works. A full gym, dumbbells at home, or bodyweight only — tell me what you have and every session is built with it.

What's the guarantee?

The rebuild-free guarantee: if the plan doesn't fit your schedule, equipment, or body at any point in the 12 weeks, tell me and I rebuild it at no cost.
