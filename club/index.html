The Story

Built With a Full-Time Job

I'm not a genetic freak, and I don't train four hours a day.

But in the last fourteen months, on a system built around a real schedule, I took my marathon from 4:45 to 3:27 on the same course. I ran 37 miles with 8,000 feet of climbing through the mountains. Two weeks later I was on a track running quarter-mile repeats at a pace I couldn't touch in high school.

This summer that same system took my mile to 5:36 and my 5K from 20:02 to 19:37 — in a single week. Not because I found more time. Because I stopped training randomly and started training like an athlete again — running that builds an engine, lifting that builds a frame, mobility that keeps a desk-job body moving like it did at 18.

That system is what I coach. And now it's what you can train on, every single month.

No — the Club is group programming. How much time does it take?

45–60 minutes a day, five to six days a week. Built for a person with a job, not a pro with a nap schedule.

It's built for exactly you. Every program has an on-ramp tier, and the strength work scales from bodyweight up. You start where you are.
