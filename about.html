Coached By Someone Who Gets It

Coach Tyler Reddy
Founder · Always Reddy Athletics

I'm Tyler. And I know exactly what it feels like to look in the mirror and not recognize yourself.

I grew up training. Started young, fell in love with the process, and built my whole identity around it. Training wasn't something I did — it was something I was. Then it stopped. Life got busy, work responsibilities grew. I looked up a few years later and realized I'd quietly let the most important part of my identity go.

Getting it back wasn't just about losing weight or getting back in the gym. It was about deciding who I was again. The training was the vehicle. The identity was the destination.

I built ARA because I know there are thousands of people in the same position — people who still feel like athletes on the inside, but whose bodies and habits stopped reflecting that. I used this exact system to drop 1:18 off my marathon time on the same course in under a year, rebuild my strength from the ground up, and get back to training like my results actually mattered. Then I used it to help others do the same. Every single athlete I've coached has hit a personal record. That's not a coincidence — that's the program working.
