Privacy Policy
Effective Date: April 19, 2026 · Last Updated: April 19, 2026

Always Reddy Athletics is operated by Tyler Reddy as a sole proprietorship based in Pennsylvania. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Submit a coaching application through our Google Form
  • Schedule a discovery call through Calendly
  • Contact us directly via email or social media
  • Make a payment through Stripe or join our community through Skool

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, athletic background, fitness goals, health information, and payment details. We do not store payment information directly — all payments are processed securely through Stripe or Skool's payment processor.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Review and respond to coaching applications
  • Deliver the coaching services and community programming you have enrolled in
  • Communicate with you about your program, progress, and scheduling
  • Send relevant updates, resources, or offers related to Always Reddy Athletics (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve our programs and services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party platforms to operate our business. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Forms — for coaching applications (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Calendly — for scheduling discovery calls (calendly.com/privacy)
  • Stripe — for payment processing (stripe.com/privacy)
  • Skool — for community access, membership billing, and cancellations (skool.com/privacy)

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to deliver our services and comply with legal obligations. If you would like your data deleted, please contact us at alwaysreddyathletics@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

5. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies directly beyond standard analytics. Third-party services embedded in or linked from our pages (such as Google Forms, Calendly, and Skool) may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at alwaysreddyathletics@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
