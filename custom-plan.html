Questions.
How long until I get my plan?

3–5 business days after your form and payment are both in. If your race is coming up fast, mention it in your notes and we'll prioritize it.

How is this different from the static PDF plans?

Those are templates built for a general fitness level. This is built from your actual PRs and goal race, and it's delivered as a living page — it tracks your training, computes your paces, and adjusts to your specific timeline instead of a one-size-fits-all download.

Can I upgrade to check-ins later?

Yes. Reach out anytime through the contact page and Tyler can add check-ins to an existing plan.

What if my race is more than 10 weeks out?

No problem — just tell us your race date on the form. We'll size the plan to fit your actual timeline.

Do I need a specific watch or app?

No. Any watch or phone works — you just log your paces and mileage right on your training page as you go.
